# Changelog

## 0.6.0
- Initial public release.
- Valkyrie's Hatchery: a buildable fish hatchery (Crafting tab) with 4, 6, 8, or 12 storage slots.
- Fish Roe: sold by Haldor, or made at the food preparation table from any fish.
- Hatching: one roe at a time, biome-weighted, with other biomes' fish unlocked by boss kills. Catches up while you're away; stops when full.
- Fishing skill XP when you collect hatched fish.
- Server-synced settings.
